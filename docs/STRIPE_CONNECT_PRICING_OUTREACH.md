# Stripe Connect Pricing Outreach

## When To Send

Send this outreach after both conditions have been met for two consecutive months:

- At least five paying ScaleSafe merchants have connected Stripe.
- Combined monthly Stripe payment volume through ScaleSafe is at least $100,000.

Before sending, replace every bracketed field with current figures.
