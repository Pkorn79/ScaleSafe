Product and installation

What is ScaleSafe?

ScaleSafe helps high-ticket and mixed-ticket service businesses collect enrollment, payment, fulfillment, communication, and engagement evidence throughout the customer relationship. It organizes those records for operations and chargeback response.

Does ScaleSafe replace GoHighLevel?

No. ScaleSafe runs inside a GoHighLevel sub-account and uses GHL for selected contacts, communication, workflow, and fulfillment activity.

Is ScaleSafe installed once per agency?

Each merchant installation is bound to a specific GHL sub-account. An agency may install ScaleSafe in multiple sub-accounts, but their records remain isolated.

When should I run Provisioning Health?

Run it after the merchant information, enrollment funnel domain, snapshot assets, workflows, custom values, and intended processors are configured. It is a final setup check, not the first setup step.

Payments and processors

Which payment channels are supported?

The active beta channels are Stripe, NMI, and Whop. Available actions differ by processor and by the payment or subscription identifiers returned by that processor. FanBasis checkout remains deferred until certification.

Does ScaleSafe hold merchant funds?

No. Stripe and NMI payments settle through the merchant's connected accounts. Hosted channels such as Whop collect payment through their own checkout.

Does ScaleSafe store full card numbers?

No. ScaleSafe uses processor-hosted or tokenized payment methods and shows only appropriate masked details.

Can I refund Stripe, NMI, and Whop payments?

An eligible payment can be refunded when ScaleSafe has the processor payment identifier required for that action. ScaleSafe should record the refund only after the processor confirms it.

What is Payment Reconciliation?

Reconciliation flags records that are difficult to prove against processor truth, such as missing subscription IDs, overdue billing, duplicates, unassigned payments, and recent failures. It does not silently repair records or move money.

Why is a payment unassigned?

ScaleSafe received the payment but could not tie it to one exact enrollment. A Quick Manual Sale with no offer intentionally creates this type of client-level payment.

What is the difference between installments and a subscription?

Installments divide a fixed program price into a defined number of payments. A subscription renews until it is cancelled. The offer and processor record must reflect the intended structure.

Clients, consent, and evidence

What evidence can ScaleSafe collect?

Evidence may include consent, accepted terms, payment history, milestones, pulse responses, appointments, attendance, messages, files, service access, course progress, resources, refunds, cancellations, and verified third-party activity.

What does Linked mean in the Evidence timeline?

ScaleSafe has tied the record to one exact enrollment. That match allows the record to support the correct client journey and disputed transaction.

What does Link to Program mean?

The activity belongs to the client, but ScaleSafe does not yet have a safe, unique enrollment match. It remains client-level context and should not be treated as proof for an unrelated program.

Does ScaleSafe choose the newest enrollment when matching is unclear?

No. Multiple possible enrollments are ambiguous. ScaleSafe must not assign evidence to the newest program merely because it is newest.

Is a scheduled appointment proof of delivery?

No. A scheduled appointment is engagement evidence. An attended or completed appointment is stronger delivery evidence.

What happens when a pulse response requests follow-up?

The Dashboard surfaces the response so the merchant can act. The response also remains part of that enrollment's evidence timeline.

Is the Evidence Readiness score a win prediction?

No. The current score is a contact-level indicator of collected evidence. It does not predict whether a bank will decide a dispute in the merchant's favor.

Defense and account health

Does ScaleSafe prevent every chargeback or guarantee a win?

No. A customer can still file a chargeback, and the issuing bank decides the outcome. ScaleSafe helps reduce avoidable disputes and improves the merchant's ability to present organized, relevant evidence.

What does Needs Review mean?

ScaleSafe could not safely treat the packet as submission-ready. The transaction may not match one exact enrollment, required evidence may be missing, or the AI draft may have been unavailable. Read and correct it before submission.

Does ScaleSafe submit every defense automatically?

No. Compilation and submission are separate. Submission depends on processor support and the merchant's authorized workflow.

Is the response deadline always exact?

No. ScaleSafe may initialize a deadline using the card network's maximum window. The processor can require an earlier response, so enter the exact due date shown in the processor portal.

Why can Stripe Risk Health look extreme in a sandbox?

Test accounts often contain few transactions and many deliberate dispute tests. A small denominator can create an unrealistic rate. Treat it as test data, not a prediction.

Integrations and security

Can ScaleSafe collect evidence outside GHL?

Yes. Evidence Connections support verified external activity. The current beta foundation includes GHL Fulfillment, Zoom, and the Custom Software API, with named integrations released in stages.

Do merchants repair every unmatched integration event?

No. Automatic matching should publish only when there is one defensible enrollment. Ambiguous events are an integration-health issue for ScaleSafe support, not a recurring merchant cleanup task.

Can one merchant see another merchant's records?

No. ScaleSafe derives the merchant location from trusted authentication and keeps tenant-owned records scoped to that location.

Are integration credentials visible after connection?

Sensitive credentials are encrypted or hashed as appropriate and are not displayed in normal merchant workflows.
