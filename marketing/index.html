What gets captured

A defense packet is only as strong as its evidence.

ScaleSafe assembles the exact artifacts banks weigh when deciding a dispute.

Click-wrap consent

The exact terms accepted, with IP and timestamp — proof the buyer agreed to your refund and cancellation policy.

Payment trail

A clean record of authorization and settlement through your own NMI or Stripe account — amounts, dates, and method.

Communications

Onboarding and follow-up touchpoints logged — evidence the relationship was real and the service was rendered.

Delivery & access

Sessions attended, materials accessed, milestones hit — the record that the customer received what they paid for.

Dispute response

Everything compiled into a structured packet matched to the dispute reason code and submitted in minutes.

Risk & health signals

Early-fraud warnings and chargeback-ratio monitoring so you can act before a processor does.
